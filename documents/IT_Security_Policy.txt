1. INTRODUCTION
---------------
This document outlines the IT Security Policy for TechCore Solutions. All employees, contractors, and third-party vendors must comply with this policy. Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

2. PASSWORD POLICY
-------------------
All user accounts must comply with the following password requirements:

Minimum Requirements:
- Minimum length: 12 characters
- Must contain at least one uppercase letter
- Must contain at least one lowercase letter
- Must contain at least one number
- Must contain at least one special character (! @ # $ % ^ & *)
- Password must not contain the username or company name
- Password must not be one of the last 10 previously used passwords

Password Management:
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days
- Passwords must never be shared with anyone including IT staff
- Passwords must never be written down or stored in plain text
- Passwords must never be sent via email or instant messaging
- Use the company approved password manager LastPass for storing passwords
- IT staff will never ask for your password over the phone or email

Default Account Passwords:
- All default passwords on new devices and systems must be changed immediately upon deployment
- Default credentials on network equipment must be changed before connecting to the network

3. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION
--------------------------------
MFA is mandatory for all user accounts accessing company resources.

MFA Requirements:
- All Microsoft 365 accounts must have MFA enabled
- All VPN connections require MFA
- All cloud-based systems require MFA
- All privileged administrator accounts require hardware MFA tokens

Approved MFA Methods:
- Microsoft Authenticator app (preferred)
- SMS one-time password (allowed but not preferred)
- Hardware token (required for admin accounts)
- Email OTP is not an approved MFA method

Setting Up MFA:
1. Visit aka.ms/mfasetup
2. Sign in with your Microsoft 365 credentials
3. Choose Microsoft Authenticator as the authentication method
4. Download the Microsoft Authenticator app on your mobile device
5. Scan the QR code displayed on screen
6. Enter the verification code shown in the app
7. Click Done to complete setup

If MFA Device is Lost:
1. Immediately contact IT Helpdesk on extension 1001
2. IT will temporarily disable MFA and assist with re-registration
3. A new MFA setup must be completed within 24 hours

4. ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
--------------------------
Company IT resources including computers, networks, email, and internet are provided for business use. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work duties.

Permitted Activities:
- All work-related tasks and communications
- Limited personal browsing during breaks
- Accessing personal email during lunch breaks
- Online banking for personal use during breaks

Prohibited Activities:
- Downloading or installing unauthorised software
- Visiting websites containing illegal, offensive, or inappropriate content
- Downloading pirated software, music, movies, or other media
- Using company resources for personal business or financial gain
- Accessing or sharing confidential data without authorisation
- Disabling or bypassing security software
- Using company devices for cryptocurrency mining
- Accessing dark web or anonymous networks using Tor

Software Installation Policy:
- Only IT-approved software may be installed on company devices
- Users do not have local administrator rights to prevent unauthorised installations
- Software requests must be submitted to IT via a service request ticket
- IT will evaluate and install approved software within 48 hours

5. DATA CLASSIFICATION
-----------------------
All company data must be classified into one of four categories:

Public:
- Information that can be freely shared externally
- Examples: marketing materials, public website content, published reports
- No special handling required

Internal:
- Information for internal use only not intended for public release
- Examples: internal policies, procedures, staff directories
- Must not be shared externally without management approval

Confidential:
- Sensitive business information that could harm the company if disclosed
- Examples: financial reports, contracts, business strategies, client data
- Must be encrypted when stored or transmitted
- Access restricted to authorised personnel only
- Must not be stored on personal devices

Restricted:
- Highly sensitive information requiring strictest protection
- Examples: HR records, legal documents, security credentials, health data
- Encrypted at rest and in transit at all times
- Access requires explicit written approval from department head
- Access logged and audited regularly

6. EMAIL SECURITY
------------------
Email is the most common vector for cyberattacks. Follow these guidelines at all times.

Phishing Recognition:
- Be suspicious of unexpected emails asking you to click links or open attachments
- Check the sender's actual email address not just the display name
- Hover over links before clicking to see the actual destination URL
- Be suspicious of urgent language pressuring immediate action
- Legitimate companies will never ask for passwords via email
- Be suspicious of emails with poor grammar or spelling

Reporting Phishing:
1. Do not click any links or open any attachments
2. Click the Report Phishing button in Outlook toolbar
3. If you accidentally clicked a link, immediately call IT Security on extension 1002
4. Do not delete the email until IT Security has reviewed it

Email Encryption:
- All emails containing confidential or restricted data must be encrypted
- Use Microsoft 365 Message Encryption by typing ENCRYPT in the email subject line
- Alternatively click the Encrypt button in Outlook before sending

Email Attachment Policy:
- Do not open attachments from unknown senders
- Be cautious with attachments even from known senders if unexpected
- Attachments allowed: PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images
- Never open executable files (.exe, .bat, .cmd, .vbs, .ps1) received by email

7. DEVICE SECURITY
-------------------
All company devices must meet the following security requirements.

Endpoint Protection:
- Microsoft Defender Antivirus must be active and up to date at all times
- CrowdStrike Falcon sensor must be installed and running
- Devices must be enrolled in Microsoft Intune
- Full disk encryption using BitLocker must be enabled on all Windows devices
- Screen lock must activate after maximum 5 minutes of inactivity
- Devices must have automatic Windows updates enabled

Physical Security:
- Never leave devices unattended in public places
- Always lock your screen when stepping away from your desk (Windows key + L)
- Never allow unauthorised persons to use your device
- Never connect to unsecured public Wi-Fi without using the company VPN
- Report lost or stolen devices immediately to IT Security on extension 1002

Mobile Device Policy:
- Company email and data on personal mobile devices requires Intune enrolment
- Personal devices enrolled in Intune must have a PIN or biometric lock enabled
- Remote wipe will be applied to lost or stolen enrolled devices
- Jailbroken or rooted devices are not permitted to access company data

8. REMOTE WORK SECURITY
-------------------------
Additional security requirements apply when working remotely.

VPN Requirements:
- Company VPN must be connected at all times when accessing internal resources
- VPN server: vpn.techcore.com
- VPN client: GlobalProtect
- Do not split tunnel VPN - all traffic must route through company VPN

Home Network Security:
- Ensure your home router firmware is up to date
- Use WPA3 or WPA2 encryption on your home Wi-Fi
- Change your home router default password
- Do not allow others to use your work device on your home network

Public Wi-Fi:
- Never connect to public Wi-Fi without VPN enabled
- Avoid performing sensitive work tasks on public networks even with VPN
- Use mobile data hotspot as a safer alternative to public Wi-Fi

Screen Privacy:
- Use a privacy screen filter when working in public places
- Be aware of shoulder surfing in public locations
- Do not conduct confidential calls in public areas

9. INCIDENT RESPONSE
---------------------
All security incidents must be reported immediately regardless of severity.

What Constitutes a Security Incident:
- Suspected phishing email that was clicked
- Suspected malware or virus infection
- Unauthorised access to systems or data
- Lost or stolen device
- Accidental disclosure of confidential information
- Unusual system behaviour suggesting compromise
- Ransomware or encryption of files

Reporting Process:
1. Do not attempt to fix the issue yourself
2. Immediately call IT Security on extension 1002
3. For after-hours emergencies call +971 50 123 4567
4. Preserve all evidence - do not turn off or restart the device
5. Document what you observed including time and nature of the incident
6. IT Security will guide you through next steps

Incident Response Timeline:
- P1 Security Incident: Response within 15 minutes, 24/7
- P2 Security Incident: Response within 1 hour during business hours
- P3 Security Incident: Response within 4 hours during business hours

10. THIRD PARTY AND VENDOR ACCESS
-----------------------------------
Third party vendors requiring access to company systems must comply with this policy.

Vendor Access Requirements:
- All vendor access must be approved by the IT Manager in writing
- Vendors must sign the Third Party Security Agreement before access is granted
- Vendor accounts are temporary and must have explicit expiry dates
- All vendor sessions must be monitored and logged
- Vendors must use multi-factor authentication
- Vendor access is limited to systems required for their specific engagement

Remote Vendor Access:
- Vendors must connect through the designated vendor VPN portal
- Screen sharing sessions must be conducted through approved tools only
- Vendors must not store company data on their own systems
- All data transferred to vendors must be encrypted

11. COMPLIANCE AND AUDITING
-----------------------------
TechCore Solutions conducts regular security audits to ensure policy compliance.

Audit Activities:
- Monthly review of user access rights
- Quarterly password policy compliance check
- Semi-annual vulnerability assessment
- Annual penetration testing by approved third party
- Continuous monitoring through CrowdStrike and Microsoft Defender

Non-Compliance Consequences:
- First offence: Written warning and mandatory security training
- Second offence: Formal disciplinary action
- Third offence or severe breach: Termination and potential legal action

Security Training:
- All employees must complete annual security awareness training
- New employees must complete training within their first week
- Training is delivered through the company LMS at training.techcore.com
- Phishing simulation tests are conducted quarterly
